FAQs

We Know Switching Cleaners Is A Big Decision. Here's Everything You Need To Feel 100% Confident With Causlye.

1.How safe is it for food environments?
Causlye is 100% food-safe when used as directed. It's non-toxic, biodegradable, and leaves no harmful residue—so your kitchen stays spotless and compliant.
2.Will it damage stainless steel or surfaces?
No. Causlye is engineered for commercial kitchens and tested on stainless steel, tiles, and chimneys. It dissolves grease without corroding or scratching surfaces.
3.What if I'm not satisfied?
Zero risk. If you don't see spotless results, you're covered by our Results or Refund Guarantee—no questions asked.
4.Who is it for / not for?
Causlye is designed for restaurants, cafés, hotels, and any heavy-oil kitchen. It's not recommended for households or light cleaning—it's simply too powerful.
5.How soon will I see results?
Instantly. Spray, wait a few seconds, wipe—and watch grease vanish. No long waiting times, no scrubbing marathons.
